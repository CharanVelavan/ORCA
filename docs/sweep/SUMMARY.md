# Policy sweep summary (7 Nov 2026, night; gap ids as in Gap Register v3.1)

India was checked against 18 seed questions (SD) and a 35-item outside checklist (CL) built from NIS2, the EU Space Act proposal (a proposal, not law), NIST IR 8270/8401, US SPD-5, ENISA, CCSDS and 3GPP.
Gemini agents: B built the checklist; D searched the Indian sources twice (11, then 6 new). Claude checked every quote against the PDFs, overrode two agent verdicts, and found that the TCSR file was the draft (fixed; see goldfish-round1.md).
COVERED = a binding, in-force duty answers yes. UNCLEAR = guidance, advisory, unstated status, a power not a duty, or narrow scope. NOT_FOUND = not in the sources we checked, as of 2026-11-07.

**Totals:** UNCLEAR 40, COVERED 8, NOT_FOUND 5 (of 53). Every non-covered item maps to a gap or is parked with a reason.
