# ORCA-NTN traffic, density and coverage parameter report

## Summary

The data card is a useful starting point, but most public sources do not publish Chennai-specific, hourly service demand, cell density, or operator backhaul mix. The report separates **standards-based models and published aggregate data** from **explicit simulation assumptions**. The assumptions below are candidate generator settings, not claimed measurements of Chennai networks.

For traffic, public evidence supports diurnal variation and an evening mobile-data peak, but generally does not provide the detailed Chennai hourly curves or event multipliers requested. TRAI reports Indian usage totals and explains that busy hours differ by network element, location and day. Use those sources to calibrate broad totals; treat the hourly shapes and event multipliers below as assumptions until local traces are available. [TRAI 2023–24 performance indicators](https://trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-09/Report_14082024.pdf), [TRAI discussion of busy-hour variation](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-11/IDEA_11_08_2017.pdf)

**Conventions:** “Assumption” means a proposed synthetic-generator value without a directly supporting Chennai statistic. Peak-to-mean ratio (PMR) is peak hourly demand divided by the 24-hour mean hourly demand. Distribution and event multipliers are parameters to fit or vary in scenarios, not official values.

## Open questions

1. What geographic boundary and area does the “about 50 urban sites” inventory represent? Without that, site density and users-per-site cannot be interpreted.
2. Which radio generations, carriers, bandwidths and antenna configurations should the emulator represent? These determine path loss applicability, noise bandwidth, link capacity and load.
3. Are the services intended to model access-network demand, application-server transaction demand, or both? UPI/card transaction rates should not be mistaken for radio traffic volume.
4. Can the project obtain anonymized Chennai hourly service traces, drive-test data, or operator/venue estimates? These would be needed to calibrate traffic curves, active users, SINR and congestion.
5. Can Tamil Nadu ERSS or Chennai Police provide hourly 112 totals and actionable-call fractions? Public material located here does not provide zone-hour counts suitable for this generator.
6. What customer and terminal specs should be used for the VSATs (antenna size, EIRP, `G/T`, bandwidth, satellite/orbit, modem/modulation, target availability)? Without them, a meaningful numeric C/N or rain-margin claim is not possible.
7. Which licensed microwave bands, radio models and availability targets should be represented? Should urban rain attenuation be considered for each candidate frequency in addition to multipath fading?
8. Should Chennai city traffic be localized using publicly available transport/land-use data, or should the OMR commuter matrix remain explicitly hypothetical?
9. How should payday be defined for the synthetic calendar: common month-start proxy, multiple employer pay cycles, or an externally supplied calendar?
10. Should festival/event multipliers represent additional generated demand, or merely redistribute users and traffic across zones while preserving a citywide total?
