## 2. What Is Solid (High Confidence, Ready for Evaluation)

1. **Primary Indian Statutory Evidence (`kb/india/records.yaml` + Auto-Checked Staged Rules):**
   - **GAP-IN-09 (Satellite Operator Cyber Duties):** Extremely strong contrast between the telecom regime (`IN-AUTHZ-2026-R54-1`, `R60-1`, `R60-1-b`, `R62-2`, `R62-7`, `R59-4` + `IN-NCCS-ITSAR-G4-2-12-10`) and the space-side regime (`IN-PIB-2026-SPACEFW-ADVISORY` / `IN-SPACEFW-2026-SDLS` + staged `inspace-ngp-2024` pp. 20 & 110), where space-segment cyber rules remain voluntary guidelines and DoT portal notices (`R59-4`, `R62-7`) carry no numeric clock.
   - **GAP-IN-01 (Incident Reporting Clock Clash):** Rock-solid multi-instrument evidence across 6 different Indian clocks: CERT-In (`IN-CERTIN-DIR-2022-v`, 6h from noticing), DoT TCSR (`IN-TCSR-2024-7-1-a`, 6h from awareness + 24h update), DoT CTIR (`IN-CTIR-2024-9-1-e`, 6h from occurrence + 3h on CNE request + 24h logs), TRAI QoS (`IN-TRAI-QOS-2024-4-1-3`, 24h SNO outage + 72h RCA), plus staged CEA Power Sector Guidelines (`in-cea-cyber-power-guidelines-2021`, 24h occurrence) and RBI PSO Directions (`in-rbi-psos-cyber-resilience-2024`, 6h).
   - **GAP-IN-02 (Downstream Dependants Warning):** Very strong finding combining the absence of a downstream duty in `IN-CERTIN-DIR-2022-v` / `IN-TCSR-2024-7-1-a` with the **affirmative non-disclosure restriction in Authz Rule 37(5) (`IN-AUTHZ-2026-R37-5`)**, which requires prior written Central Government approval before sharing network information with anyone other than equipment suppliers/manufacturers.
   - **GAP-IN-08 (Backup Path Integrity):** Strong combination of `IN-CTIR-2024-5-1-a` (remote access documentation) and `IN-AUTHZ-2026-R60-1-b` (gateway in India) with staged `in-ctir-2024` Rule 5(1)(f) (physical redundancy) and `in-rbi-psos-cyber-resilience-2024` (DR site periodic testing), proving that Indian telecom law requires physical redundancy and Indian gateway location without requiring clean-path cryptographic/anomaly verification before failover.
   - **GAP-IN-03 (Containment vs. Continuity Precedence):** Clear statutory tension between containment powers (`IN-TCSR-2024-4-5`, `IN-TELACT-2023-21-b`, `IN-CERTIN-DIR-2022-iv`) and emergency continuity expectations (`IN-TELACT-2023-20-2-a`, `IN-AUTHZ-2026-R17-1-a`), strengthened by staged DoT Unified Licence Clause 41.1 (`in-dot-unified-licence` p. 29) and NDMA Guidelines (`in-ndma-communication-guidelines` p. 187).
   - **GAP-IN-04 (Tower Battery Backup Floor):** Crisp cross-sector contrast within India itself: CEA (Technical Standards for Connectivity to the Grid) Regulation 8(11) (`IN-CEA-2020-8-11`) mandates a **10-hour battery floor** for grid communication equipment, whereas TRAI QoS (`IN-TRAI-QOS-2024-3-2`) excludes power failures from cell-availability uptime and DoT Disaster SOP (`IN-DOT-SOP-2017-3-1-3`) merely says *"Ensure battery backup at vital towers"* without a numeric hour floor.
   - **GAP-IN-06 (Space Data Link Crypto Not Mandated):** Solid pairing of `IN-SPACEFW-2026-SDLS` (CCSDS 355.0-B-2 / 355.1-B-1 in a non-binding guideline) and `IN-TELACT-2023-19` with staged `in-dot-telecom-standards-rules-2025` Rule 5(1) (mandatory compliance with notified ITSARs, showing the exact legal hook DoT can use once a space-link ITSAR is notified) and `intl-3gpp-tr-33700-29` Clause 4 (gNB onboard satellite security architecture).
